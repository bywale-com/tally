# Gate 4 — Economics to ~$3k net / 30h

## Layers (must not be collapsed)

1. **Premium** the contractor pays  
2. **Brokerage commission** the carrier/MGA pays the firm (typically % of premium)  
3. **Producer split** of that commission  
4. **Tax / unpaid quoting time**

Canadian commercial P&C brokerage commission (carrier → firm): Liberty Mutual Canada publishes **15–20%** on GL/property/package. US/general P&C producer commentary: agency 10–15% of premium; producer **35–50% of new**, **20–40% of renewal**. Treat producer splits as **industry ranges, not an Ontario snow-contract**.

https://www.libertymutualcanada.com/intermediary-compensation-practices/

**UNKNOWN:** actual split at any snow-capable GTA brokerage for a new Level 1.

## Worked ranges (illustrative, not a job offer)

Assume new-business producer take-home ≈ **premium × 15% × 40% = 6% of premium** (optimistic mid). Renewal ≈ **premium × 12% × 25% = 3% of premium**.

| Account class | Sourced premium band | $ at 6% new | Accounts / month for ~$3.5k gross | Notes |
|---------------|----------------------|-------------|-------------------------------------|-------|
| Residential / light mix | James Inwood Oakville blog $1.6k–$8k | $96–$480 | 8–35 | Not the crisis book; 30h quoting still real |
| Chutter-class min | ~$15k min premium (dump) | ~$900 | ~4 | Specialty, documentation-heavy |
| Fraser-class (Oshawa, 2021) | ~$52k | ~$3,100 | **~1** | Matches named pain |
| Thacker-class (Toronto, 2021) | ~$110k | ~$6,600 | **0.5** | Harder to place; 5yr/$200k screens |
| Sim-class | ~$205k | ~$12,300 | **0.25** | SIR/large; may already be locked |

T4 vs commission: if W-2/T4 employee, $3k **net** needs more than $3k gross (ON tax + CPP/EI). If ~30% effective, need ~$4.3k gross → ~1.4 Fraser-class new accounts/month or ~5 Chutter-mins.

**Renewals will not save January.** A $50k account at 3% is $1,500/year. A $3k/month renewal book is ~24 such accounts — years of production, not 13 weeks.

## 30 hours

A snow MGA submission wants: site list, contracts, GPS/logs, loss runs, broker declaration. That is not a 20-minute BOP. Even one Fraser-class file can eat a week of a junior’s time plus Principal Broker review. Four $15k files/month in 30h while unsupervised-in-practice is not evidenced.

## Verdict

**Mathematically possible** if the book is **Fraser-class+** and the split is in the 40% new-business neighbourhood.  
**Not validated.** Small residential snow ($2–8k) almost certainly **fails** the 30h test.  
**Kills the plan** if the only available work is inbound personal lines / CSR at salary below the target, which is what most new-L1 ads look like.
