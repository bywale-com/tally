# Gate 6 — Sole occupation / Secondary Business Exemption

## The rule (not trivia)

O. Reg. 991 s. 5: the **only** business or employment of an individual licensee is insurance broker (or broker + life/A&S), unless QR Committee grants an exemption.

RIBO FAQ: “Your role as a RIBO-licensed broker must be your **primary** job or employment. If you wish to engage in other work or work part-time, you need to apply for a Secondary Business Exemption.”

https://www.ribo.com/licensee-resources/frequently-asked-questions/  
https://www.ribo.com/licensee-resources/broker-standards/guidance/ribo-guidance-005-sole-occupation-requirement-for-individual-licensees/

Guidance 005 (published **6 Feb 2026**):

- SBE **before** any secondary occupation. **New licence applications accepted only after the exemption is granted.**
- Principal Broker letter: activity will not interfere; they fully support.
- Form asks: hours/week in the **broker** business; commission or salary; hours and pay in the secondary occupation.
- QR looks at conflict, coercion, duties, **hours**, **remuneration**.

## How the user’s other life maps

| Activity | Auto-approved? | Note |
|----------|----------------|------|
| Unpaid full-time student | Likely **not “employment”** | Not listed. Confirm with licensing@ribo.com. Paid TA/job **is** employment. |
| Tutoring | **Approved** (Instructors: Tutors) | If that’s all school-adjacent pay |
| Professors | Approved with a note | Not the user’s status |
| Omcoda / Tally / other client-facing product or sales work | **Not auto-approved** | Incompatible/QR list includes **“IT Client-Facing Sales/Advisory Services”** and **“Sales/Advisory Services.”** Running a sales-methodology company while selling insurance is exactly the conflict pattern QR exists to stop. |
| Reduced-hours broker as *secondary* to Omcoda | **Fails the primary-job rule** | Brokerage must be primary. The 30h cap makes “primary” awkward unless other paid work is small. |

Unmanageable-conflict occupations are **refused**. Even then you may apply; it is not a shortcut.

## Reduced-hours outbound-heavy

The SBE form and FAQ assume broker work is the main occupation. A Principal Broker who signs “30h hunter, also runs another business, also in school” is taking QR and E&O risk. Public ads are full-time. **No sourced example** of a new Level 1 approved for this shape.

## Verdict

**Hard gate, currently leaning fail** unless:

1. Brokerage is the **primary paid** occupation, and  
2. Omcoda/Tally is paused, made non-client-facing, or QR-approved in writing **before** the licence is issued, and  
3. School is confirmed as non-employment or separately exempted.

This can kill the plan **even if** a snow-capable firm would otherwise hire.
