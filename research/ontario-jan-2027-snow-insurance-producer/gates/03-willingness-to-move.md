## Pain ≠ switching

What is **not** evidenced:

- A 2026 owner saying they **moved broker** and it helped.
- A 2026 owner willing to let a **new Level 1** quote mid-term.
- That shopping a hard market produces a cheaper or even *available* alternative. Hard markets often mean “your current specialty broker is the only one who will write you.”

Marsh/SIR/HortProtect already occupy the association channel. Incumbent relationships + three-year loss runs + Totten five-year screen all raise switching friction.

Ken Sim (2021): GPS/logs **already used**, premiums still rose. That is dissatisfaction with **insurers**, not proof they will fire the broker.

## Verdict

**Pain: pass (2026).**  
**Mobility: UNKNOWN.** This is the remaining PMF hole. Do not treat “premiums are high” as “they will move to you.”

**Cheapest test:** 15 LO snow/landscape owners: “When did you last change broker? Would you let someone new quote this renewal? Who places you now?” Count movers vs “we already have Marsh/specialty.”
