# Gate 1 — Sponsorship to originate commercial

## What must be true

RIBO Level 1 cannot sell independently. By-Law No. 3 s. 3.3: a Level 1 must (i) act under a Principal Broker and (ii) be employed or sponsored by an Active Firm Licensee. Licence application requires an employment confirmation letter signed by the Principal/Deputy Principal Broker (dated within 10 days). Employment must be obtained within one year of passing the exam.

https://www.ribo.com/getting-a-license/individual-licenses/new-applicants/license-application-form/license-application-page-3/

RIBO’s own licence-level page: Level 1 brokers “typically handling personal and **less complex commercial** insurance policies with **limited independence**.” Complex/specialty commercial is described as Level 2 territory.

https://www.ribo.com/getting-a-license/individual-licenses/upgrading-your-license/license-levels/

Snow CGL with $10k BI deductibles, additional-insured contracts, and MGA applications is not “less complex commercial.” Legal: a Level 1 *can* work files under supervision. Practical: the Principal Broker must *allow* origination of this class.

## Clock (licence, not competence)

- Exam: 100 MC, 75% pass, $325/attempt, monthly IBAO/IIC sittings through 18 Dec 2026. Informal grade 1–2 days; official ~2 weeks (IIC).
- Course: IBAO Broker Launchpad on-demand $580–$640; weekend live Sep 19–Oct 4 2026 still listed. Study commonly 60–120 hours.
- Licence fee: $240 current year; Oct 1 2026–Sep 30 2027 annual $265. Jul–Sep applications pay partial + next year.
- Processing: 5–10 business days if complete; **new licence applications accepted only after any required SBE is granted** (Gate 6).
