# Gate 2 — Carrier / MGA access for snow and landscape

## What must be true

The **sponsoring brokerage** must already be able to place the class. A Level 1 with no firm appointment cannot submit to Totten.

## Markets still writing (2025–2026)

Affinity / not a wholesale feed:

- Marsh Canada / HortProtect — CNLA and Landscape Ontario endorsed business insurance (still live 2026).
- LO SIR group — larger contractors; **not new operators**; Unica historically tied to SIR (2023 LO policy report).

Standard personal-lines carriers (Intact, Economical, etc.) were reported as having **exited or restricted** snow management (LO policy report; names as of 2023). Do not assume a personal-lines brokerage can place this class.

## Verdict

**PASS at industry level.** Capacity exists in 2026 (SWG still on the record in February; Unique Risks still marketing in 2026; Totten program still listed).

**UNKNOWN at firm level** until a specific brokerage confirms appointments. A random RIBO shop is more likely to **decline** snow than to have Totten/SWG/Unique codes.

Totten’s five-year / $200k screen also means the **easy** outbound list is established contractors, not startups — which matches the prior PMF quotes (Thacker, Fraser, Sim) and is good for ticket size, bad for “anyone with a plow.”
