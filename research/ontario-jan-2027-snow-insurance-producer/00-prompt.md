# Prompt (source of record)

Date: 2026-09-29

Test viability of **placing** snow/landscape contractor insurance as a RIBO Level 1 producer (not underwriting it).

Aside from getting licensed and becoming competent, six external things must exist:

1. A brokerage willing to sponsor you and let you originate commercial business (cannot independently sell after Level 1).
2. That brokerage must have carrier/MGA access for snow and landscape risks.
3. Enough contractors genuinely dissatisfied and **willing to move**.
4. Brokerage economics that can produce ~$3,000 net/month in ≤30 hours (premium × commission % × producer split × account count).
5. Sales cycle short enough for January (Oct–Jan renewals or urgent placement).
6. Employment structure that legally and operationally tolerates school / Omcoda / other work (RIBO sole-occupation / Secondary Business Exemption).

Exact test:

> Does there exist an Ontario brokerage with snow/landscape markets that will sponsor a new Level 1 producer, allow a reduced-hours outbound-heavy structure, and pay enough per placed account that a reachable number of Oct–Jan contractor renewals gets you to ~$3k net/month?

If yes, this is a real plan. If no, it dies immediately.
