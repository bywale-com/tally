## How this market is structured (read first)

Three different kinds of firms show up in snow/landscape CGL search results. Mixing them up produces false “I can submit to Totten” conclusions.

| Kind | What they are | Who can submit / buy |
|---|---|---|
| **MGA / MGU** | Wholesale underwriting: Totten (now under SPG Canada), SWG Specialty, Unique Risks, Chutter | Licensed **retail brokers**. Contractors do not buy direct. |
| **Affinity / program retail** | Marsh Canada (HortProtect / CNLA / Landscape Ontario endorsement; also Greenius/LMN); IPMGA / The Insurance Market around SIR | **Contractors** (usually members / screened group). Not a wholesale market other brokers feed. |
| **Retail brokerages** | GTA independents advertising snow/landscape pages | Contractors as clients. A Level 1 would place *through* one of these, not around them. |

RIBO Level 1 is legally a general P&C broker licence under a Principal Broker. It is **not** a wholesale appointment, an MGA code, or a snow-program underwriting ticket.

**Facts**

- Totten describes itself as an MGA that “supports the broker community.” SPG Canada’s 1 Jan 2025 structure is “delivering personal and commercial P&C insurance solutions **to licensed insurance brokers** across Canada.”
- Snow program (as indexed 2026): commercial and residential snow; auxiliary landscape/hardscape; **five years continuous snow operations**; **$200,000 minimum revenue** (all operations); copies of contracts, site list, GPS/logs, carrier loss runs; CGL $2M–$10M; BI deductible **minimum $10,000**.
- New submissions: `triage@tottengroup.com` / 1-888-868-8367.
- The application has a **BROKER DECLARATION** that “each and every question must be answered by the Broker and/or Account Executive,” plus “Complete Name and Address of Insurance Brokerage” and dual signature (Insured + Broker). That is broker-channel paper, not a contractor walk-up form.
- Broker onboarding is a **firm** registration: SPG form asks for brokerage details, head office, accounting contact, brokerage principal. BD: `businessdevelopment@spgcanada.ca`.
- **Who can submit: licensed retail brokers (after brokerage appointment / SPG registration). Not the public. Not an unlicensed person. A Level 1 at a RIBO firm could in principle submit only if that firm is appointed and a supervising broker signs.**

**UNKNOWN:** whether Totten still requires five years / $200k on every file in winter 2026–27 (page is live in search; Cloudflare blocked a full fetch). Whether a newly appointed tiny brokerage gets snow capacity on day one.

## 4. Is a brand-new Level 1 typically allowed to originate specialty commercial (snow) vs personal lines / CSR?

### What RIBO actually authorizes

- Level 1 = “entry-level brokers working **under the supervision of a Principal Broker**.” Must be employed/sponsored by an active RIBO firm. https://www.ribo.com/getting-a-license/individual-licenses/new-applicants/level-1-licensing/
- Levels: 1 entry; **2 “more advanced technical and commercial insurance expertise”** still under a PB; 3 principal. https://www.ribo.com/getting-a-license/individual-licenses/
- Licence is not split PL vs CL. Training vendors state Level 1 can sell home, auto, **and commercial** under supervision (e.g. https://mrdconsulting.ca/licensing/ ; https://pnclearning.com/2020/06/26/ribo-license-the-ultimate-guide/ ). That is **legal scope**, not a job description.

### What brokerages actually hire new Level 1s to do

Pattern from 2025–2026 ads + IBAO:

1. **Personal lines CSR / AM / inbound sales** is the default door. HUB HCC (auto/property, leads provided, they pay for RIBO). OTIP home & auto. Mowen’s junior-adjacent seat is **Personal Lines AM** with a **1-year CSR** floor. IBAO (2022, still the association’s published talent note): college fair “CSR” and “Account Manager” postings were **the same job**. https://tob.ibao.org/industry-and-markets/7-insights-on-talent-in-the-broker-channel/
2. **Commercial service (junior AM)** is the next door, and it already wants **1–3 years commercial servicing** (My Insurance Broker). Title says junior; bar is not “passed Level 1 yesterday.”
3. **Commercial producer / hunter** ads at named firms want **1–2+ years producing** (Mitch 2 years; Mowen CAE 1 year commercial; Experia proven mid-market; Element 2 years). Hello World will talk to “junior producers” but prefers some industry experience and **hides the employer**.
4. **Paid commercial trainee cohorts** exist (Zensurance CBTP 2024 and 2025; HUB trainee aggregator 2026 unconfirmed). Those seats are **inbound small-commercial** on a house platform, not “go originate Totten snow files.” Zensurance’s own snow URL is a contractor-facing product page; the trainee ad never says snow/landscape specialty.
5. **MGA snow desks do not hire Level 1 producers to bind their programs.** Totten/SWG/Unique/Chutter are broker-only wholesale. A new Level 1 cannot “get a Totten code” as a person; the **brokerage** gets appointed.

### Specialty snow specifically

- Totten published appetite: **five years’ snow operations** and **$200k** receipts — that is the **insured**, but it also signals the files are documented, loss-run-heavy, contract-heavy. Chutter Ontario **$15k minimum premium** and **$350k+** revenue. Marsh HortProtect (2023): **three years’ insurance history**. SIR: **not new operators**. Unique Risks will write **100% snow** — still via appointed brokers, guidelines unpublished.
- A brand-new Level 1 originating that class would need (a) a RIBO firm that already has MGA codes, (b) a PB who lets a trainee handle high-severity CGL, (c) contractor relationships. **No 2025–2026 ad found that combines those three.**

**Typical, in one sentence:** new Level 1s are hired into **supervised personal lines or inbound commercial service**; specialty snow/landscape CGL is placed by **experienced commercial people at appointed retail shops**, or by **Marsh/IPMGA program staff** — not by a solo new licensee submitting to MGAs.

**UNKNOWN:** unpublished internal policies at Mowen, Oracle, Shepherd, Roughley, J D Smith on whether a new Level 1 may *assist* on snow files. Reddit colour (r/InsuranceCanada, 2025) is anecdote: many start unlicensed/reception/CSR; commission-only producer seats chew people up. https://www.reddit.com/r/InsuranceCanada/comments/1n3fchu/getting_a_position_as_an_insurance_broker_with_no/

IBAO Launchpad: ~**60%** of Level 1 students are **already employed** by a brokerage (hire-then-license). That is the common sponsorship direction: **firm hires the person, then registers the licence** — not “MGA sponsors a producer to go hunt snow.” https://tob.ibao.org/industry-and-markets/7-insights-on-talent-in-the-broker-channel/ and https://ibao.org/become-a-broker/how-to-get-started
