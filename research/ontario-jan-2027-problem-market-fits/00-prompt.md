# Prompt (source of record)

Date: 2026-09-29

> **Find problem-market fits, not just markets.**

Each candidate must already have enough evidence to support:

- a specific reachable buyer
- a repeated, salient lived problem
- a current method they already use
- an experienced consequence/cost
- dissatisfaction with that method
- a clear desired outcome
- a congruent January-accessible technical mechanism
- existing willingness to pay / pricing evidence
- unit economics that can plausibly yield **≥$3,000 net/month within ≤30 hours/week**
- enough market size / recurrence / buyer density to make that reliable by January 2027

Do not admit a market merely because it is large, fragmented, technical, or has high ticket prices.

Do not invent scenarios from regulations, vendor claims, rate cards, or plausible pain.

A candidate only survives when **problem-market fit + delivery feasibility + unit economics + January access** are all sufficiently evidenced.

The research goal is:

> **Produce a shortlist of evidence-backed problem-market fits that I could realistically capture by January 2027, with validated economics and explicit remaining uncertainties.**
