# PMF candidate: A tradesperson who actually shows up for the small building

- Slug: landlord-cant-get-trades
- Verdict: KILL
- Killed at: problem
- Buyer (reachable): Self-managing small landlord; boutique PM. Phone lists exist (Brampton RRL, CMRAO, FRPO).
- Outcome (no mechanism nouns): The repair happens in a time the operator already considers reasonable, without becoming the GC.
- Mechanism (January-legal): Whatever legal trade the job is — **this fish is coordination/reliability, not a single ticket.** January-open slices only (locksmith, mechanical doors, ELV). Cannot be 309A/G.2/CFAA as the delivery.
- $3k net / 30h path (one paragraph, after tax/unpaid time): A general “I am the guy who shows up” across compulsory trades fails January. Inside open trades it still needs a repeating felt problem. One confession is not a path to $3k.
- Remaining uncertainties (explicit): How many small landlords still have an empty vendor bench vs a “full stable.”

## Problem-market fit evidence

### Lived problem (quotes + URLs)
Need ≥2 independent same-segment first-person accounts.

**Only one landlord confession of the actual sentence:**

> “I'm a private landlord, and I would have LOVED to have run into you when I first bought my investment townhouse. I now have a full stable of contractors … but it's been a struggle to find reliable trades.”  
> https://www.reddit.com/r/OntarioLandlord/comments/1v8my0z/where_do_property_managers_find_contractors/

**Second landlord, same thread — not the same problem:**

> “Landlord here, I find pretty much all of my contractors through word of mouth or google and get quotes for larger jobs. I tend to stay away from full service companies as they tend to charge rates that would render the units not profitable.”

That is **method they use** (Google/WOM) plus price objection to full-service shops — not “nobody will come.”

**Tenant-narrated PM delay (wrong mouth; alarm-bounce analogue):**

> “after lots of pestering they let me know they found an electrician on the 30th. It took until July 10th for them to book a time, but the electrician never showed up. … the only answer I got … the electrician they picked is all booked up.”  
> https://www.reddit.com/r/OntarioLandlord/comments/1v2r121/no_light_in_kitchen_for_nearly_1_and_a_half/

Vendor platforms (KRU, United Collective, PropCare, Rentra) selling “contractor roulette” — not first-person buyers.

### Current method
One landlord: now has a “full stable” after a struggle (past-tense toward a solved bench). Other: Google + WOM, avoid full-service. Tenant’s PM: one booked electrician, no-show.

### Consequence experienced
Tenant: 1.5 months without a kitchen light. Landlord cost not stated by the landlord. “Struggle” is not a dollar, a day, or a missed rent.

### Dissatisfaction with that method
The Google/WOM landlord is dissatisfied with **full-service pricing**, not with finding people. The “struggle” landlord already has a bench.

### Outcome
Repair done without the owner living on hold.

### Congruence check
A January-legal specialist (doors, locks, ELV) is **not** congruent with “I need a plumber/electrician who shows up” — those doors are closed. A handyman-of-all-trades is a different, unlicensed-scope mess. Tenant electrician no-show is not PM voice.

## Delivery / January
Cannot deliver the evidenced adjacent pain (licensed electrical) by January. Open trades were hunted as separate fish and also died.

## Willingness to pay / prices (URLs)
No ticket attached to “show up.” Full-service rates are what one landlord **refuses**.

## Unit economics
Not run. Heterogeneous jobs destroy 30h if it becomes on-call GC.

## Density / recurrence / reliability by January 2027
Thousands of small landlords is pond size. One Reddit sentence is not recurrence.

## What we are not claiming
Not that hiring trades is easy. Not that October calling 15 boutique PMs is a waste — that is still the empirical test. Internet evidence is below the bar.
