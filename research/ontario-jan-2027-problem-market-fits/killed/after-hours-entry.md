# PMF candidate: After-hours, someone with a key actually comes

- Slug: after-hours-entry
- Verdict: KILL
- Killed at: dissatisfaction
- Buyer (reachable): Boutique PM / super of walk-ups and student buildings (the people who already run an after-hours line).
- Outcome (no mechanism nouns): The occupant gets in tonight without the building staying unsecured.
- Mechanism (January-legal): Locksmith or super with keys; not fire/electrical.
- $3k net / 30h path (one paragraph, after tax/unpaid time): The $319 after-hours invoice shows a ticket exists. Sitting on-call for lockouts is the same 24/7 packaging that can destroy a 30h week (unpaid wait + drive at 02:00). RTA s.134 also fights passing a “fee” to tenants. Economics would likely fail even if PMF held.
- Remaining uncertainties (explicit): How many boutique PMs already have a $50–$150 on-call super vs hire a locksmith.

## Problem-market fit evidence

### Lived problem (quotes + URLs)
Lockouts happen. The **buyer** does not say the current method fails them. Evidence is tenant-side.

> “on september 1st, my previous sublet got locked out of the building after hours… they told her it would be a 2 hour wait as the technician lived out of town, and that she would be charged. she immediately declined… almost 3 months later, i am being presented with a $319.25 invoice”  
> Description: “Technician dispatched / Tenant called back to cancel call after technician had already left”  
> https://www.reddit.com/r/OntarioLandlord/comments/1h0t5ec/being_charged_exorbitant_afterhours_lockout_fee/

> Notice that building will charge $50 “due to a large number of people locking themselves out.” Comment: “Management has to call someone in, that someone get paid OT to deal with locked out tenants.”  
> https://www.reddit.com/r/OntarioLandlord/comments/1pgwswe/landlord_to_start_charging_fee_to_let_people_into/

Overlaps locksmith file: landlords DIY rekey; LTB locksmith **did** attend in 48 hours (Sharif v Lodhi $423.75).

### Current method
After-hours phone line → dispatch technician or OT super. Method **worked** in the $319 story (tech was already rolling). Building is adding a fee because volume is high — that is a pricing response, not “we cannot get anyone.”

### Consequence experienced
Tenant: 2-hour wait, then a disputed bill. Operator: OT labour (asserted by a commenter, not a named PM). No lost night of operations from a plaza/PM first person.

### Dissatisfaction with that method
Tenants hate the fee and cite RTA s.134. Commenters argue the landlord **cannot** charge for own time. That is WTP **blocked**, not the operator wanting a different dispatch method. No PM: “I hate ABC Lock at 2am and I would switch.”

### Outcome
Person gets in; door is not left unlocked.

### Congruence check
A faster locksmith is congruent with “2-hour wait.” The evidenced complaint is **who pays**, not “please be a different method.” Same-night lockout also collides with ≤30h.

## Delivery / January
Locksmithing open.

## Willingness to pay / prices (URLs)
$319.25 after-hours invoice (disputed). $50 building fee (legality contested). Matrix lockout card $70–$220. https://matrixlocksmith.ca/locksmith-price-list-toronto/

## Unit economics
On-call lockout: unpaid waiting + GTA drive. Unlikely to net $3k in ≤30h as the whole book. Not rescued.

## Density / recurrence / reliability by January 2027
Student-building lockouts look recurrent. Recurrence of **operator willingness to pay a new method** is not evidenced.

## What we are not claiming
Not that after-hours entry never happens. Not that a boutique PM call in October couldn’t surface a different sentence. This pass: no PMF.
