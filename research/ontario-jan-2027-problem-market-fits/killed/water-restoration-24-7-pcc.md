### Current method
Call the 24-hour insurer line. Get assigned a preferred restoration contractor. Wait in the catastrophe queue. Photograph everything. Sometimes DIY demo while waiting. Sometimes later take a cash settlement because the assigned firm is distrusted (Onside Homestars / no-show).

### Consequence experienced
Basement gutted; cars flooded; days-to-weeks with no contractor; DIY demo; quote held 33 days; mould smell while waiting (Ottawa commenter running fans); claim buried if you only used the app.

### Dissatisfaction with that method
Explicit with **the assigned contractor / insurer speed**, not with “I wish I had known IICRC S500.” Onside no-show. Ottawa reassignment to the bottom of a new list. Mississauga “have not heard back.”

### Outcome
The house dry and rebuilt, insurer paying, without sitting in sewage/water for days.

### Congruence check

**Mechanism can dry the house.** That is not enough. The scenario they affirm is: **“I already called Intact/Belair at 2:30am / Canada Day / overnight and I am waiting on their contractor.”** A congruent product is a **panel truck that answers tonight**, or a cash-out rebuild GC — not a weekday 30h independent WRT route.

Taking cash and hiring “your own company” (RFD 2264115; Onside thread) still starts as an **insured emergency**. Rebuild (drywall, cabinets, laminate) is not WRT drying. See also cash-Category-1 kill: the non-panel slice does not independently clear the bar.
