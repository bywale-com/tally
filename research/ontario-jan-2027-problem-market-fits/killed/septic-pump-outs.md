# PMF candidate: tank emptied before sewage is in the house

- Slug: `septic-pump-outs`
- Verdict: KILL
- Killed at: dissatisfaction (Ontario owners **already have a guy**; tickets too small for 30h even if they did not)
- Buyer (reachable): rural/cottage and GTA-fringe homeowners (Georgina, Uxbridge, Caledon, Kawarthas). Core TCH/Toronto walk-ups are **sewer**, wrong species.
- Outcome (no mechanism nouns): toilets work; no sewage on the floor
- Mechanism (January-legal): pump-out is a hauled-sewage/ECA world, not the Part 8 BCIN installer exam. Legal to haul with the right waste approvals — **not** a 13-week “I bought a vacuum truck and a phone.” Install/bed work freezes in the lock-in window (hunting-ground G9).
- $3k net / 30h path (one paragraph, after tax/unpaid time): lived prices **$100–$300** every 2–5 years (RFD). At $300/job, **10 jobs/week** before dump fees, drive, and unpaid selling. GTA-fringe density does not support that inside 30h. Install tickets were not sourced as a January product.
- Remaining uncertainties (explicit): QuARTS/OOWA counts for York/Peel rural still unpulled. Would not reverse dissatisfaction/economics even if the count is large.

### Current method
Call “my septic guy” every 2–5 years; sometimes yearly for peace of mind; dig the lid yourself to save the uncover charge.

### Consequence experienced
One self-caused backup after dumping 50 gallons. Routine $100–$225 invoices. Not a standing overflow.

### Dissatisfaction with that method
**Fails.** “Small price to pay”; “so far, all has been good”; neighbours “no big problems. Periodic pump outs is all.”

### Outcome
Tank not full; field not used as a cistern.

### Congruence check
A new pumper **can** empty a tank. They are not looking for one. Re-inspection letters as inbound remain untested and would still be a $200-class SKU.

## Delivery / January
Pumping continues in winter; **installs** do not. Truck + ECA + dump fees are not a cheap reversible start. BCIN installer exam is weeks but **wrong season** for beds.

## Willingness to pay / prices (URLs)
Lived $100 / $175 / $225 / ~$130 inspect+pump. Vendor $300–$600. Either way **below** a $3k/30h unit unless extreme routing (unshown).

## Unit economics
Fails on ticket × density × travel. Do not rescue with Part 8 replacement TAM.

## Density / recurrence / reliability by January 2027
GTA urban core is municipal sewer. Recurrence is multi-year. January lock-in on **pumping** would be a thin rural route, not a GTA book.

## What we are not claiming
Not claiming Ontario has no septic stock (~paired with 500k wells provincially). Claiming **no PMF**: owners are not dissatisfied, tickets are small, geography fights GTA, January fights installs.
