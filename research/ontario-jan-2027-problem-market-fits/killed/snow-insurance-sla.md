Pay the new premium or exit; shop specialty brokers; SIR pool (~12 large contractors); LO standard-form contract; GPS/time-stamped logs; over-apply salt; pass cost to customers; Bill 118 60-day notice (already law, premiums still high in the quotes).

### Consequence experienced
$16k→$52–60k; $5k→$110k; $70k→$205k; 15% of revenue; $20k–$100k deductibles; shops closing for no coverage; near-closure (Fraser).

### Dissatisfaction with that method
Explicit. Sim: tracking software **did not** stop the increase. Thacker: documentation **did not** stop the increase. That is dissatisfaction with **insurers’ pricing**, not with “I lack a log app.”

### Outcome
