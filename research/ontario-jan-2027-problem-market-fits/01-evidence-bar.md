# Evidence bar

This layer is **Deep Screener Layer 2 applied to a service the operator would sell**, plus delivery, economics, and January access. Hunting-ground admission (`../ontario-jan-2027-hunting-grounds/`) is **necessary, not sufficient**. A large pond with no evidenced fish is not a shortlist item.

## What counts as evidence

| Required | Counts | Does not count |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| Reachable buyer | Named species + how you phone them (directory, licence list, civic address) | “PMs exist”; “landlords exist” |
| Lived problem | ≥2 independent first-person or same-role accounts, present-tense, self-affirmable, same segment | Vendor blog; “they must struggle”; one anecdote; statute |
| Current method | What they say they do now | What a vendor says they should do |
| Consequence | Cost, delay, hit, fine, lost day they **experienced** | Hypothetical fine maxima; “could hit a gas line” |
| Dissatisfaction with **that method** | They want a different method, not just a different universe | “Ontario One Call is a joke” attached to a private-locate product (incongruent) |
| Outcome | Stated without product nouns | Mechanism sale (“I locate private plant”) |
| January mechanism | Legal + learnable in 13–18 weeks; congruent with the outcome | CFAA/309A/G.2 as the only delivery path |
| WTP / price | They already pay this class of ticket, or comparable jobs in the same segment | “I could charge $150/h”; category rate cards with no buyer of this problem |
| Unit economics | Path to ~$50k profit in ≤30h including unpaid time | Billed revenue = take-home |
| Density / recurrence | Enough repeating buyers/jobs by January for reliability, not a one-off | TAM of an industry |

## Ladder

Fail any of: problem-market fit → delivery → economics → January access. Stop. Do not rescue with a prettier TAM.

UNKNOWN is allowed. Invented first-person scenarios are not.

## Congruence (hard)

The mechanism must deliver the outcome the evidenced problem is about.

Public One Call wait is **not** private-locate PMF.  
Fire Code existence is **not** monthly-CTI PMF.  
Tenant-disabled smoke alarms are **not** emergency-lighting PMF.  
Working cameras / privacy fights are **not** “one person owns the NVR” PMF.

## Output

- Survivors: `candidates/<slug>.md`
- Failed attempts: `killed/<slug>.md` with the gate that died
- Empty shortlist is a valid result
