# Hunting-ground admission gates

A **market / hunting ground / pond** is a set of reachable buyers plus existing spend plus a fragmented service supply. It is not a slogan, not a capability, not a rate card, and not a Deep Screener scenario.

Admit only if the pond is worth **October calling**. Kill if the pond is too small, closed, commoditized-by-platform, or economically unable to yield ~$50k profit inside 30h. Mark UNKNOWN rather than inventing.

## Gates (all must pass or be UNKNOWN with a named cheap test)

| ID | Gate | Pass looks like | Kill looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Size for one tiny operator | ~$50k annual profit is << available economic pool (order-of-magnitude: <1% of a documented spend/revenue pool, or thousands of independent buyers) | Niche with a handful of buyers; $50k would be a noticeable share |
| G2 | 30h economics | Incumbents already transact at economics that can yield ~$3k net in ≤30h after SE tax, unpaid time, insurance, travel (typically ~$60–$100+ delivered hour or equivalent ticket) | Category is $25–40/h labour, or 40h packaging only, or utilization structurally poor |
| G3 | Reachable buyers | Identifiable operational humans (owners, PMs, shop principals) who can be named, counted, and phoned without a platform | Buyers only exist inside marketplaces, tenders, or hospital/municipal RFPs |
| G4 | Price/unit already paid | Public quotes, contractor forums, association guides, or job tickets showing the band — not “could charge” | No evidence customers pay that band in this pond |
| G5 | January technical access | A legal, competent path in 13–18 weeks without a multi-year ticket **or** cheap reversible first step before spending $2–8k | ESA C of Q, CFAA registered-by-January, 4th-class OE, etc. as the only door |
| G6 | Heterogeneous problems | Several distinct jobs-to-be-done exist in the pond (so October search can kill fish without emptying the pond) | One imagined scenario is the entire market |
| G7 | Fragmentation | Many small suppliers; a new tiny operator is not blocked by 1–2 incumbents or a platform taking distribution | USIC/One Call monopoly, Uber, OEM-only dealer networks |
| G8 | Concentration | Buyer side not a few accounts; supplier side not a cartel | 3 GCs control all spend |
| G9 | Seasonality | Can lock income by January **or** use Oct–Jan as search/relationship season with a named winter/spring cash path | Pond is closed in the lock-in window with no offsetting winter work or book-build path |
| G10 | Regulatory | Barriers named; unlicensed/unregistered work that is actually illegal is not the plan | The only paid work is licensed and the license clock overruns January |

## Evidence rules

- Cite URLs, reports, association pages, StatsCan, municipal licence counts, rate cards, job ads **for market structure**.
- Price and regulation prove **pond economics / barriers**, not problem-market fit of a proposition.
- If a number cannot be sourced, write UNKNOWN and the cheapest test (count Yellow Pages / HCAI / Landscape Ontario members / business licences) — do not fabricate TAM.
- Do not construct first-person scenarios. If buyer-voice appears, file it under “problem-class hypotheses, untested.”

## Verdicts

- **ADMIT** — all hard gates pass; remaining UNKNOWN items are cheap to resolve in-market. Eligible for problem discovery.
- **KILL** — a hard gate fails. Do not call into it in October as the primary pond.
- **UNKNOWN** — missing data that would change admission. Name the test. Do not admit on vibes.
