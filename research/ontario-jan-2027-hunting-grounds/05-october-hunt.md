# October hunt — empirical, not a pitch

Admitted ponds are the **search space**. The scanner analogue: a good hunting ground plus a ruthless elimination mechanism. This file is the mechanism. It is not a business plan.

## Rules

1. Call into an **admitted** pond only (or an UNKNOWN cheap test).
2. Do not pitch a product. Do not educate them into a need.
3. Kill fish in one conversation. Keep the pond.
4. Spend $0 on credentials and gear until a fish **repeats unprompted**.
5. Stop a pond if ten calls produce no repeating complaint and no repeating paid vendor gap.

## What to log (same skeleton, no mechanism yet)

For each call:

- Who (role, firm, city)
- Pond (which file)
- What they actually do when X happens (method)
- Whether that method is fine
- Anything they volunteered as repeating pain
- Whether they already have a somebody
- Whether they are reachable again

Do **not** write a Deep Screener scenario from one colourful quote.

## Suggested order (certainty of the pond, not of a fish)

1. **Outdoor contractor owners** (Primary 1) — densest named list, already $75–150/h, winter path attached.
2. **Plaza supers + boutique PMs** (Primary 2) — year-round buildings.
3. **Door shops / locksmiths** (Primary 3) — mystery-shop supply *and* walk plaza docks.
4. **ELV/cabling shops** (Primary 4) — only after 2–3, because the buyer is often the same building.
5. **Restoration independents** (Primary 5) — only to test cash vs 24/7 packaging.
6. **UNKNOWN tests** in [04-unknown.md](04-unknown.md) if capacity remains — especially private locates *as a question to outdoor owners*, not as a startup.

## What “winner” would look like later

A problem class that:

- repeats across independent calls in one pond,
- has a burned current method,
- maps to a January-legal mechanism,
- and can be stated as an outcome without naming the tool.

Until that exists, stay in **market certainty + proposition uncertainty**. That is the point of this folder.
