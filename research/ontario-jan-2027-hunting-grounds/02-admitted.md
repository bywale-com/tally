# Admitted hunting grounds

These ponds cleared the boring gates (size, reachable buyers, existing $60–$100+ economics, January-legal door, fragmentation, seasonality, $50k microscopic vs pool). **They are not products.** Proposition uncertainty remains. Deep Screener has not been run.

Treat the list as **phone lists**, not 16 businesses. Several files are slices of the same pond.

## How to read this

- **Primary hunting grounds** = distinct reachable populations worth October calling.
- **Attached paths** = winter cash or specialist trucks that live on the same list.
- **Thin / product-shaped** = economics and legality pass, but heterogeneity is mostly SKUs. Do not let these become the whole search.
