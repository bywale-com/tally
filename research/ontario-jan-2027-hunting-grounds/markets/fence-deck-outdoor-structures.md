- Slug: `fence-deck-outdoor-structures`
- Pond type: Independent fence, deck, pergola/rail contractors plus the linear-foot and per-sq-ft tickets they already sell to homeowners (and some commercial sites).

## What this pond is

Fence installation sits in **NAICS 238990** (all other specialty trade contractors) and is also done by 56173 landscapers. Listing databases put **~750–1,200** Ontario fence contractors, **~150** in Toronto proper, mostly single-owner. 2026 GTA installed fence tickets cluster **$3k–$12k** for a backyard; decks **$6k–$25k+** depending on size/material.

ISED 2389 (parent of both site prep and “all other specialty,” including fence) Ontario 2025: **16,926** establishments (5,819 employers + 11,107 non-employers). That parent is much larger than fence-only; do not treat 16,926 as fence TAM.

No “sell permits/quoting software to fence guys.” No claim that HomeStars reviews equal demand. No first-person homeowner scenario.
