## What this pond is

A very large **legal-person** market: 13,318 Ontario condo corporations, ~933k residential units, 46k directors. They spend real money on building systems. Most day-to-day buying is delegated to **licensed condominium management providers** (402 firms). A minority are self-managed. The Condo Registry names directors and managers but is restricted to personal use.

## What we are not claiming

- Not that condos lack money or problems.
- Not that a proposition could never be sold into condos **through managers** (that is a different pond).
- Not a Deep Screener kill of “compliance CTI”; this is a **reachability** kill of **boards as the October call list**.
