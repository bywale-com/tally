- Verdict: ADMIT
- Confidence in verdict: medium

## What this pond is

An Ontario/GTA **security systems industry**:

- **Demand:** every commercial and multi-res site that already buys cameras, enterphones, fobs, and monitoring — the same reachable humans as the small-building buyer ponds.
- **Supply:** hundreds of small installers/integrators plus a smaller set of ULC-listed monitoring stations. CANASA is the association directory.
- **Price evidence** is advertised commercial install and monitoring bands, used only as economics.
- **Intercoms / enterphones** sit here: same buyers (walk-ups, condos, plazas), same shops, no separate enumerated buyer list. Maglock-on-exit is **not** a casual intercom add-on; it is a permitted life-safety alteration.

## What we are not claiming

- Not “sell CCTV to landlords” as a screened proposition (already Layer-2 killed as a **product**; the **industry** still exists).
- Not that unlicensed 120 V work is a January plan.
- Not that a new ULC central station can be stood up by January.
