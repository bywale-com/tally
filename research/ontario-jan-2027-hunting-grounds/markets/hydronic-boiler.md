## What this pond is

Mostly a **named subset of the HVAC/plumbing pond**, not a separate hunting ground. Residential gas boilers sit inside G.2 scope. Piping/hydronic distribution sits inside 306A/307A. The shops that answer the phone are often the same TSSA-registered heating contractors or plumber-fitter firms. January access fails for the same ticket clocks as HVAC.

## What we are not claiming

No “in-floor leak detection” product. No claim that hydronics is higher-margin than forced air (unsourced). No claim it is *smaller* than HVAC — share is UNKNOWN.
