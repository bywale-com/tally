## What this pond is

A large, fragmented GTA field-service market. Independent shops already charge diagnostic and repair tickets in the $90–$350 common-repair band and higher for motors/boards. Buyers are homeowners and light-commercial occupiers. Supply is many TSSA-registered, HRAI-affiliated independents plus some larger mechanical contractors. Gas-side work is G.2/G.1 plus contractor registration. Refrigerant-side work is 313A or 313D plus an ODP card to *handle* refrigerant (the card is not the trade licence).

## What we are not claiming

No invented proposition (no “after-hours no-heat subscription,” no “filter club”). No claim that wages or apprenticeship pay packages the 30h constraint. No claim that G.3 helper work is market ownership.
