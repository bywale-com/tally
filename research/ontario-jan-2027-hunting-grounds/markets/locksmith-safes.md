## What this pond is

Ontario locksmithing is a named but non-compulsory trade. Buyers already pay published GTA tickets in the $70–$600 band for entry, rekey, hardware, and safes. Security-guard/PI licensing is a different statute and does not gate locksmiths.

## What we are not claiming

No “be the honest locksmith vs $15 ads” proposition. No claim that auto lockout at 30h yields $50k after drive time. No claim that access-control/alarm install is unlocked by the locksmith exemption.
