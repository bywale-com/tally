## What this pond is

A large, already-transacting Ontario/GTA market in which water losses are mitigated and dried, often documented to IICRC S500 and billed in Xactimate, sometimes via insurer preferred-contractor panels, sometimes as cash jobs. Technician certification (WRT, often ASD/AMRT) is a short course, not a multi-year ticket. Tiny GTA operators already advertise as founder-led IICRC shops.

## What we are not claiming

No invented “IICRC solo drying route.” No claim that a 30h week is compatible with Intact 60-minute SLA work. No claim that T4 restoration wages package to $3k net in 30h — they do not.
