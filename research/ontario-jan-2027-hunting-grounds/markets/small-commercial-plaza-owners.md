## What this pond is

Neighbourhood **strip plazas** as a buyer + spend + supply set:

- Toronto staff counted **412** strip plazas, concentrated in Scarborough and North York, mostly 5–9 bays, supporting 3,000+ establishments.
- Buyers are typically private owners or small local companies and the super who holds the vendor list. BOMA Toronto is the CRE association surface but is biased to **>25,000 sq ft** principal members — useful for larger strips, incomplete for the small-bay stock.
- Existing spend is ordinary commercial building ops (doors, fire, grease hoods for restaurant tenants, cameras, snow). Vacancy is tight in neighbourhood/strip product, so sites are operating, not mothballed.

## What we are not claiming

- Not “BOMA is the list of small plaza owners.”
- Not a count of GTA-wide plazas (Toronto-only 412 is the hard number).
- Not that Fire Code existence means a super will pay for a new category.
