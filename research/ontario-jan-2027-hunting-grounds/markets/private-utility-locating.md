## What this pond is

A **commercial** layer beside the public One Call system: firms with EM locators and GPR that invoice for private plant (site lighting feeds, private water/gas, secondary buildings, etc.) and for concrete scanning. Public rate cards in this layer are **~$125–$175/h** with **two-hour-ish minima**. The **public** LSP layer is killed separately (`utility-locating-one-call-lsp.md`).

Technician *employment* at public LSPs ($25–$43/h) is **not** this pond’s owner-operator rate card.

## What we are not claiming

Not arguing that private locates is the winning product. Previous Deep Screener Layer-2 kill of a private-locate *proposition* is not automatically market death — but it is also not admission. Statute ≠ demand.
