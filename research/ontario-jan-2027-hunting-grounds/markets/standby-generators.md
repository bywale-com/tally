## What this pond is

A dual-regulated install-and-service market around natural-gas (or propane) standby generators and automatic transfer switches. Published residential install prices are five figures; published annual service is hundreds to low thousands. Legal work splits gas (TSSA) and electrical (ESA). Brand channel (Generac especially) structures a lot of warranty service.

## What we are not claiming

No “storm-week dispatch” proposition. No claim that portable/inverter generators are this pond. No claim that an unlicensed oil change is a January business.
