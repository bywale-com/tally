## What this pond is

A real, large, **licensed** life-safety service industry:

- **Who holds the contracts:** electrical contractors with CERTI-FIRE/CFAA techs (ECAO RFAC lists), fire-protection companies (CASA members, many small Toronto shops), and ULC-listed monitoring stations.
- **Who is licensed:** Fire Marshal-acceptable **people** for alarm annuals/repairs (CFAA or CERTI-FIRE); **427A** for water-based and specialty suppression including kitchen systems; **ESA LEC** to contract electrical work; ULC listing to certificate monitoring.
- **January door:** coursework can start (CFAA pack ~$2.5k) but **full paid annual-inspection status** is not a 13–18 week outcome. 427A/309A overruns by years.
- Ontario **does not** run a BC-style RFPT licence for extinguisher techs; OFC still pulls in NFPA 10 “certified person” for annual maintenance. That gap is not a hunting ground by itself.

## What we are not claiming

- Not that buildings lack Fire Code duties (they have them).
- Not that a later, credentialed operator could not hunt this pond after CFAA/427A.
- Not a Layer-2 proposition kill recycled as market death — the **industry** is alive; **January access** is dead.
