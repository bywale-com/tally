- Verdict: UNKNOWN

- January access: pass for mechanical PM/repair of compressors (433A is voluntary); fail for refrigerated-dryer refrigerant work and for line-voltage installs.

## What this pond is

A real GTA industrial field-service category with **documented Mississauga independents** alongside Atlas Copco–class distributors. Millwright certification is voluntary, so mechanical compressor PM is not blocked by a multi-year compulsory C of Q. Ticket prices are unpublished. How much of the money is OEM-warranty vs all-makes independent is unpublished. Refrigerant dryers and electrical installs re-impose 313A/ESA clocks.

## What we are not claiming

No “I’ll be the independent Atlas Copco alternative” proposition. No claimed hourly. No claim that GTA independent density is sufficient — only that it is **non-zero**.
