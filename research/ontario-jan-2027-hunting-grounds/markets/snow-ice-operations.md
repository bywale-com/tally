## What this pond is

The winter half of GTA outdoor trades. Landscape Ontario treats snow/ice as a first-class sector (advocacy on liability and salt, ANSI/SIMA work, Snowposium). Public 2026 commercial pricing is **seasonal mid-four to low-five figures per site**, or **$150–$500 per push**, or **$150–$400/h** with a **$2,500** commercial minimum at one GTA operator. Some municipalities licence the activity (Vaughan **$609** initial).

This file exists so seasonality gates on landscape, excavation, fence, and irrigation can point to a **named** winter cash path rather than hand-waving.

## What we are not claiming

No “buy a plow and you’ll hit $50k.” Insurance availability and claim risk are not solved by a rate card. No first-person plaza-manager scenario.

## Problem-class hypotheses (untested, not admission criteria)

- Insurance availability/price for new operators (untested; LO says incumbents are leaving)
- Commercial contract timing (fall vs January) (partially evidenced in contractor blogs)
- Residential vs commercial packaging inside 30h (untested)
