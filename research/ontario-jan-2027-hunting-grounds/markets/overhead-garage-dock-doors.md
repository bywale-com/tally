# Market: Overhead / garage / dock doors (GTA)

- Slug: `overhead-garage-dock-doors`
- Pond type: homeowners, property managers, and warehouse/retail operators paying for sectional/rolling-door and dock-equipment repair, plus many independent door shops and some branded dealers.
- Verdict: ADMIT
- Confidence in verdict: medium
- $50k vs pool: <1% vs tens/hundreds of thousands of potential door-owning buyers (Toronto CMA 883,125 single-detached + 164,580 semis, 2021; garage presence not counted). Order-of-magnitude: $50k is a rounding error on even a conservative annual repair pool.
- Primary buyer species: homeowner; warehouse/shipping supervisor; condo/property manager; auto-shop owner.
- January access: pass (mechanical spring/cable/track/operator-swap where plug-in; fail only for new hardwired electrical)
- Seasonality: year-round; cold-weather spring failures are common shop narrative, not a closed season.

## What this pond is

A fragmented field-service market around residential garage doors and commercial overhead/dock doors. Independents already publish $280-class spring tickets and $120-class diagnostics. Mechanical work has no provincial trade ticket. Electrical hardwire is a separate ESA pond-slice and is excluded from January access for this operator. Buyer side is homeowners plus warehouse/retail/auto-shop principals.

## What we are not claiming

No “broken-spring 45-minute route” as a proven product. No claim that dock-leveler hydraulics, high-cycle commercial, or hardwired 3-phase operators are inside the unlicensed scope. No winner pick among residential vs commercial doors.
