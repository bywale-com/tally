## What this pond is

GTA owners already pay several thousand dollars for professionally ground and coated garage (and similar) slabs. It is indoor, fragmented, and not a skilled-trade C of Q. Distinct from outdoor site-work.

## What we are not claiming

No “salt-proof garage in one day” product. No claim that materials leave $100/h after grinding labour. No franchise-epoxy pitch.
