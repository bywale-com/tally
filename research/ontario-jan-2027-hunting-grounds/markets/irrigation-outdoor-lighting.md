## What this pond is

Two Landscape Ontario sectors sitting on top of NAICS 56173:

- **Irrigation**: association training (WSIP), an annual conference, published residential system tickets in the **$2.5k–$8.5k** band and **~$550/zone**.
- **Lighting**: a small but active specialist group (**20+** members, 92 symposium attendees), public GTA packages **$2.5k–$6k** typical, billed labour **$80–$120/h** on at least one Toronto specialist’s 2026 guide.

ESA is the distinctive barrier vs general landscaping: low-voltage still needs a Yard and Garden permit; 120V is compulsory electrician territory. LO’s 2025 lighting report says inspector practice is tightening and talks with ESA are ongoing.

## What we are not claiming

No “you should sell lighting design” or irrigation audits. WSIP and ESA permits prove **barriers and existing service classes**, not a winner proposition. Do not infer demand from ESA’s permit rule.
