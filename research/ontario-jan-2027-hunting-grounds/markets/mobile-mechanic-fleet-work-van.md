## What this pond is

A very large, fragmented vehicle-repair market including mobile operators who already post $85–$90/h and some who post per-van fleet PM. Ontario law treats the core work as a compulsory trade (310S). Fleet and work-van demand is economically **plausible** (downtime of a trades van is expensive) but **not evidenced as a lived, converting scenario** in this pass.

## What we are not claiming

No “work van back tonight” product. No claim that $129/van yard PM is validated demand. No claim that mobile $90/h packages $3k net inside 30h after unpaid travel. No pick of consumer-mobile vs fleet.

## Problem-class hypotheses (untested, not admission criteria)

- Trades van down overnight / next-morning route loss (untested; vendor blogs assert $500–$1,000/day downtime — treat as marketing).
- Evening/weekend yard PM so vehicles earn in the day (untested).
- Roadside no-start vs booked shop wait (untested).
