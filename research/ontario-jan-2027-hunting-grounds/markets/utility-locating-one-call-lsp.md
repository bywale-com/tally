# Market: Utility locating — public One Call Locate Service Providers (Ontario)

- Slug: `utility-locating-one-call-lsp`
- Pond type: A handful of Locate Service Providers contracted by underground infrastructure owners to fulfil Ontario One Call tickets; the notification system itself is a regulator, not a buyer.
- Verdict: KILL
- Confidence in verdict: high
- $50k vs pool: Not the constraint. Public locate *volume* is large; **addressable** volume for a new tiny operator as an LSP is near zero without a UIO contract.
- Primary buyer species: Underground infrastructure owners (Enbridge, LDCs, telcos, municipalities) via geographic LSP contracts — not a shop principal you cold-call into a job next week
- January access: fail (as LSP) / pass only as a **technician employee** of an incumbent LSP
- Seasonality: Year-round legally; volume follows excavation season

## What this pond is

Ontario One Call is a **notification regulator**. Physical public locates are performed by **infrastructure owners** or the **LSPs they authorize**. On the Locate Alliance Consortium map, Ontario is partitioned among **G-Tel, Promark-Telecon, Canadian Locators Inc. (CLI), and PVS**. Technician labour inside those firms is advertised at **~$25–$40/h**. Contracted municipal unit prices (Promark/SDG) are **~$59–$108 per locate** depending on utility count.

This is an industry with large ticket volume and a closed distribution system.
