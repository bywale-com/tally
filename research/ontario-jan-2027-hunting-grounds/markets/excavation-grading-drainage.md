## What this pond is

Canadian industry **23891 Site preparation contractors**: excavating, grading, demolition, septic, land clearing, construction equipment rental *with* operator. ISED 2024 SME: avg revenue **$734.2k**, **69.8%** profitable, profitable-firm avg profit **$137.7k**. Ontario listing databases show **~1,750** excavating contractors (POIData). GTA residential packaging is commonly mini-excavator + operator at **~$120–185/h** or **~$750 half-day / ~$1,200 day**, with project tickets (pool, underpinning, drainage) in the mid-four to low-five figures.

Toronto separately licences **drain contractors (79)** and **drain layers (90)** as of 2024 — a countable, phoneable drainage subset inside the city. Grading/excavation generally is not a Toronto Chapter 545 trade licence.

## What we are not claiming

No invented proposition (private locates, drainage design software, “be the mini-ex guy”). No claim that ICI civil tenders are reachable in 30h. Price cards prove pond economics only.
