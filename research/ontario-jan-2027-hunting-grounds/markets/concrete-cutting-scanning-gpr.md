## What this pond is

A specialty-trade industry that sells **mobilized cutting/coring** and **GPR/x-ray scanning** to construction and renovation. Public minima around **$350** and hourly **~$155–$175** for scan/locate-adjacent work; cutting often quoted per linear foot / per core with a trip minimum. Supply includes one large Canadian cutter (Ontario Cutting & Coring, Mississauga/Ottawa) and a long tail of GTA shops. Overlaps the private-locate pond on GPR but is a different buyer conversation (scan-before-core vs dig-before-shovel).

## What we are not claiming

No “sell scanning to cutters” or “be the GPR guy.” Reddit is colour. $4–$12/sq ft marketing ranges are weak evidence.
