# Market: Independent / boutique property-management firms

- Slug: `independent-boutique-pm-firms`
- Pond type: Small-to-mid property-management companies (condo and rental) that already buy building contractors, plus a fragmented vendor supply selling into those firms.
- Verdict: ADMIT
- Confidence in verdict: high
- $50k vs pool: <<1% of contractor spend flowing through hundreds of independent firms (order-of-magnitude)
- Primary buyer species: Firm principal, operations manager, or site property manager — not CAPREIT asset management
- January access: pass
- Seasonality: Year-round building operations; fiscal/AGM cycles affect condo vendors more than rental PMs

## What this pond is

Independent property-management **companies** as a reachable buyer population:

- **Condo side** is enumerated: 402 CMRAO-licensed provider businesses, public registry, ACMO directory, named principals on ACMO 2000. They sit in front of ~10,700 managed corporations (vs ~2,400 self-managed).
- **Rental side** is less enumerated: no CMRAO equivalent for MURB/house-plex managers; Ontario does not run a dedicated residential-PM licence. Directories still show thousands of “property management” establishments (quality of those scrapes is UNKNOWN). FRPO is the rental-housing membership surface.
- These firms already run vendor lists (fire, elevator, HVAC, doors, security, generators). A tiny operator’s $50k is a rounding error against that combined contractor book.
- CAPREIT-scale **owners** are excluded by design; this pond is the boutique/independent **manager** who picks the plumber.

## What we are not claiming

- Not “start a PM company by January.”
- Not that CMRAO 402 are all boutique (some are large); the **reachable remainder** is still hundreds of firms.
- Not a winning vendor category. October calling is to hear what they already buy and hate.

## Problem-class hypotheses (untested, not admission criteria)

- After-hours coverage gaps for small portfolios (untested).
- Coordinating extra-low-voltage / cameras / fobs without an ESA fire-alarm contractor (untested).
- Vendor no-shows on 6–40 unit buildings that big fire houses deprioritize (untested).
- Documentation packs for audits (RentSafeTO / Mississauga rental standards) as a paid coordination job (untested).
