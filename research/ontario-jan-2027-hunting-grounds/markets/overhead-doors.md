## What this pond is

A **distinct adjacent** pond to plazas and industrial buildings: people who own or superintend **overhead / garage / dock doors** and already buy preventative maintenance and emergency repair. Supply is many GTA door companies. Price cards show $100+ visits and parts tickets. Intercoms/access control overlap some of the same shops (Ventri, Reliance) but the **door** buyer list (docks, bays) is walkable without becoming a CCTV company.

## What we are not claiming

- Not “open a garage-door franchise.”
- Not a count of GTA commercial doors.
- Not that fire-rated door ITM is open.
