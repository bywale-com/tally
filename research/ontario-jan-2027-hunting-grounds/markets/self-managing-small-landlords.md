## What this pond is

A large, fragmented set of **owner-operators** of small rental stock in Ontario/GTA:

- Purpose-built **small structures**: CMHC apartment universe in Toronto CMA still has ~31k units in 3–19 unit buildings (units, not buildings). Walk-up / house-plex owners are the humans who pay the plumber, the door shop, and the fire contractor.
- **Secondary rental**: ~208k rented condo apartments (Oct 2024) plus a material share of rented houses/duplexes (~25% of GTA rented dwellings in 2021 BILD/Urbanation compilation of Census/CMHC). Many of these owners self-manage one to a handful of units.
- **Municipal named lists** exist for subsets: Brampton 1–4 unit RRL (6,257 licences by June 2026; 78% ARU/basement/garden/rooms); Mississauga 6+ unit / 2+ storey purpose-built (337-building pilot universe); Toronto RentSafeTO ~3,600 buildings at 10+ units (overlaps “small walk-up” only at the low end).
- **Association surface**: FRPO claims 2,200+ members owning/managing 350,000 households, explicitly including “one small building or a single rental unit,” plus a public supplier directory. CFAA in the **apartment-association** sense (Canadian Federation of Apartment Associations) is the federal counterpart FRPO sits in; it is a landlord lobby, not a fire-tech registry.
- Spend is existing building operations (repairs, life safety, entry, turnover), evidenced by contractor rate cards and mandatory inspection regimes — not by a claimed “felt demand” from Fire Code text alone.

## What we are not claiming

- Not a product. Not “become the small-landlord’s IT guy / camera guy / compliance CTI.”
- Not that every basement-apartment owner is phoneable from a clean Toronto licence file (they are not).
- Not that RentSafeTO **is** the house-plex pond (10+ units / 3+ storeys is larger).
- Not that LTB volume is a service TAM; it only shows how many landlord entities are operationally active under the RTA.
