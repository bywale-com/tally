## What this pond is

Ontario hot tubs are typically **used in January**, unlike outdoor pools. Independents already post $145–$230 diagnostic hours and $169–$350 service tickets.

## What we are not claiming

No “winter hot-tub route” product. No admission on TAM. Outdoor pool opening/closing is a different, January-dead pond.
