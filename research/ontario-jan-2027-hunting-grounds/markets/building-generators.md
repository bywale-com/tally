## What this pond is

A **distinct** adjacent building-ops pond: sites with emergency generators and automatic transfer switches, legally tied to CSA C282 and OFC 6.7. Service supply is specialist (Genrep, SGS, Apex and peers), overlapping electrical and gas credentials. Buyer list is a subset of condo/ICI/plaza PMs, not house-plex landlords.

## What we are not claiming

- Not that monthly C282 items are unpaid — some sites outsource them — only that the **legal specialist core** is ticketed and not January-open for a new operator without TSSA/ESA.
- Not a genset count or TAM.
