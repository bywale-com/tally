## Solar / EV charger / ESA electrician

- EV charger: ESA requires Licensed Electrical Contractor (ECRA/ESA) + notification of work before start. https://esasafe.com/home-renovation-buying-and-selling/ev-charging-systems/
- Only an LEC may be hired for electrical work in a home; Master Electrician cannot be hired directly. https://esasafe.com/compliance/finding-the-right-contractor/
- 309A Electrician — Construction and Maintenance: compulsory; 9,000 hours (~5 years): 8,160 on-job + 840 in-school. https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/trade-information/electrician-construction-and-maintenance/
- O. Reg. 570/05: electrical contractor must designate a Master Electrician; ME licence requires C of Q in electrician trade + ME exam. https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/050570
- Solar grid-tie is electrical work under the same LEC/ESA door (installer marketing, consistent with ESA hire-LEC rule). https://solarcalculatorcanada.org/blog/best-esa-certified-residential-solar-installers-ontario

## Locksmith / safes / auto lockout vs alarm

- PSISA s. 2(7)(i): locksmiths explicitly exempt. https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/05p34
- Locksmith 259L is a **non-compulsory** named trade; no C of Q; apprenticeship exists (6,000 on-job + 480 in-school) but is not required to practise. https://skilledtradesontario.ca/trade-information/locksmith/
- Security guard / PI **do** need PSISA licences. https://www.ontario.ca/page/security-guard-or-private-investigator-licence-individuals
- Alarm / fire-alarm is a different legal object (ESA power, Fire Code ITM) — not the locksmith exemption.
- Published GTA tickets: house lockout $70–$220; car $90–$190; business $100–$290; safe opening $140–$490; panic hardware $185–$620. https://matrixlocksmith.ca/locksmith-price-list-toronto/ Similar bands $95–$180 lockouts. https://torontopremierlocksmith.com/how-much-does-a-locksmith-cost/
- Bait-and-switch / fake locksmith channel is documented as a GTA consumer problem. https://thegtalockman.com/2025/11/08/is-your-locksmith-is-legitimate-in-ontario/
- **24/7 flag:** emergency lockout is the mass-market SKU.

## Backflow / cross-connection

- OWWA: install/repair of backflow preventers is plumber (journeyman or apprentice under journeyman) under trades law. Testing is municipal, not provincial. https://owwa.ca/certification-training/
- George Brown: 5-day seminar $1,600 including OWWA exam + 5-year registration. Tester must be Engineer, CET, journeyman plumber, plumbing apprentice, steamfitter, sprinkler fitter, irrigation specialist, **or anyone authorized by the purveyor’s by-law**. https://www.georgebrown.ca/programs/types/apprenticeship/cross-connection-specialist-backflow-tester-certification-and-re-certification
- Toronto Water: testers are plumbers, engineers, fire sprinkler fitters with CCCS. Schedule 6 of Chapter 851. Plumbing contractors need Toronto plumbing contractor licence + C of Q + OWWA/ASSE + calibrated gauge. Annual test mandatory for ICI and Part 3 residential. https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/water-supply-by-law/backflow-prevention-program/
- Vendor price band (not a by-law): annual test ~$100–$300/device, GTA quotes $120–$250. https://fixyflow.com/blog/backflow-testing-ontario

## Elevator

- Elevating Devices Mechanic 636E: 7,280 on-job + 720 in-school = 8,000 hours. TSSA EDM-T required **before** apprentice registration; offence to work before EDM-T. EDM-A exam after hours. Trade is non-compulsory under BOSTA but TSSA certificate is mandatory to touch devices. https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/wp-content/uploads/1970/01/Elevating-Devices-Mechanic-636E-TS-FINAL-May-29th-2023-ENG.pdf https://www.tssa.org/sites/default/files/2023-11/ED-How-to-become-an-EDM-A---Final---10-Nov-16.pdf
- Buyer side: TSSA-licensed contractors, building owners via maintenance contracts — concentrated (Otis, KONE, Schindler, TK, plus independents).

## 4th-class operating engineer

- TSSA: 1,920 hours practical as trainee in a rated plant, or 480 hours after a full-time TSSA time-reduction program. 33% of QET must be boiler operation. Two 150-question exams, 65% pass. Certificate within 5 years of exams. https://www.tssa.org/sites/default/files/2023-10/OE-4th-Class-CE-Guideline-Effective-June-15-2021-Final-v.1.pdf
- Packaging is plant attendance / shift work, not a 30h owned route.

## CFAA registered fire-alarm technician (standalone)

- Ontario Fire Code Div. C 1.2: persons doing annual FA tests/inspections/repairs must complete a program acceptable to the Fire Marshal. Named programs: CFAA Fire Alarm Technician Training Program; ECAO Certi-Fire. https://www.ontario.ca/page/fire-safety-legislation-and-ontarios-fire-code https://ontariofirecode.com/ontario-fire-code/ontario-fire-code/division-c-admistrative-provisions/section-1-2-qualifications/
- CFAA: 5 theory courses, 180 hours; “many students” 6 months, most 1.5–2 years; ~$3,000 online + exams. Trainee registration after courses; 1,500 hours recommended before practical exam. https://cfaa.ca/fire-alarm-technician-education-program https://cfaa.ca/fire-alarm-technician-education-program-requirements
- Seneca summary: five courses + one year work with experienced technician to apply for registration. https://www.senecapolytechnic.ca/ce/community/safety-services/fire-alarm-technician.html
- 13–18 weeks is shorter than the typical CFAA clock. Wiring/verification also collides with ESA/309A for electrical portions.
