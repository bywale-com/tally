## Ticket clocks (G5/G10 — apply across gas, refrigeration, line-voltage)

### TSSA gas (O. Reg. 215/01 + TSSA policies)

- Regulation: https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/r01215
  - s.3: no person performs certificate-holder functions without a certificate (G.1, G.2, G.3, DA, …).
  - s.6: G.1 requires valid G.2 + 2 years / 4,000 hours including 500h on >400,000 Btuh under G.1.
  - s.7: G.2 and DA require a G.3 (or operating-engineer ticket).
  - s.20–22: G.1 any input; G.2 ≤400,000 Btuh independent; **G.3 supervised only** (general supervision for limited tasks; direct supervision for full scope). G.3 shall not do initial activation of a new/converted appliance.
  - s.21(2)+s.20(2)6: gas-side of refrigerating/AC only; beyond gas-side needs refrigeration C of Q.
  - s.20(2)7: limited electrical from existing branch; no run to panel without electrician C of Q.
  - s.20(2)3: disconnect/reconnect water to swap appliance; no additional plumbing without plumber/steamfitter C of Q.
  - s.31 DA: unvented residential gas appliances or vented refrigerator ≤100,000 Btuh (not construction heaters). Not furnaces/boilers.
- TSSA policies (hours): https://www.tssa.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/FS-Gas%20Technician%20or%20Oil%20Burner%20Technician%20Policies%20and%20Procedures-2025.pdf
  - G.3 modules 1–9: **180h**.
  - DA: G.3 + modules 11–16: **240h additional / 420 cumulative**.
  - G.2: G.3 + modules 10–24: **480h additional / 660 cumulative**.
  - G.1: + modules 26–31: **140h / 800 cumulative**.
- From 1 Jan 2026: G.3-by-challenge holders need **450h G.3 work** before G.2 enrolment. https://www.tssa.org/new-requirement-fuels-g2-enrolment-proof-g3-work-experience-challenge-certificate-holders
- Conestoga (evening, TSSA-accredited): https://continuing-education.conestogac.on.ca/ProfessionalDesignations/gas-technician
  - G.3: 180h, 2 levels, **$1,396**. Level 1 Sep 1–Oct 9, 2026; Level 2 Oct 19–Dec 18, 2026 (Tue–Thu 6–10pm).
  - G.2: 420h, 5 levels × 7 weeks, **$3,303.20**. G.2 Level 1 listed **Jan 11–Feb 26, 2027**. Completing G.2 via this calendar **after** a fall 2026 G.3 lands well after January 2027.
  - G.1 GAP $2,996.17 / G.1 $4,323.92.
- NATS G.2 diploma (Brampton/London): **33 weeks, 720h, requires G.3**, tuition **$11,920**. https://nats.ca/programs/gas-technician2/
  - NATS page quotes government wage $32/h avg — **wage, not shop door**. Do not use for G2.
- Great Lakes College G.2: 404h, tuition $8,220 (search capture). https://www.greatlakescollege.ca/gastechnician2
- Contractor registration (business, not just ticket):
  - O. Reg. 212/01 s.21(1): “No person shall act as a contractor unless the person is registered for the purpose.” https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/010212
  - TSSA contractor page: https://www.tssa.org/contractor-registration-and-certification
  - How-to portal PDF: https://www.tssa.org/sites/default/files/2024-04/How%20to%20Submit%20Fuels%20Safety%20Registration%20in%20Ontario%20as%20a%20Contractor%20application%20%281%29.pdf

### Refrigeration / AC (Skilled Trades Ontario)

- 313D Residential Air Conditioning Systems Mechanic: **compulsory**, 4,500h (4,020 OJT + 480 school), ~2.5 years. https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/trade-information/residential-air-conditioning-systems-mechanic/

### ODP / ozone (not a trade licence)

- Ontario.ca: 1-day course, 75%, 5-year card. **Cannot work on or fix refrigeration equipment with only an ODP card.** Needs C of Q in refrigeration (or certain automotive trades) to repair. https://www.ontario.ca/page/certificate-handle-refrigerants
- O. Reg. 463/10: https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/100463
- HRAI ODP/ODS online: $275+HST, 30 days to complete, 1.5h exam. Explicitly **not** a trade qualification. https://www.hrai.ca/odp-ods-online-program

### ESA / electrical
